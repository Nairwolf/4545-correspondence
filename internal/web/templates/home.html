Rules & FAQ

Every Monday all active players are paired and games are created on Lichess automatically. One game per player per round. Time control is correspondence, a few days per move.

Standings use a rolling performance rating over your last few games; XP and levels reward playing regardless of result (win 3 / draw 2 / loss 1, level = √XP).

Pause any time from your dashboard — it takes effect next round and never affects games already running. Fair-play and inactivity policy follow the Lichess4545 league norms.
