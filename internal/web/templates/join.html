Join the league

Infinite Correspondence is an open-ended correspondence ladder on Lichess. Every Monday, every active member gets one new game against another member, created for you on Lichess automatically. There are no seasons and nothing to sign up for each week — you play, and the standings, performance rating and levels take care of themselves.

How joining works

  1. You continue to Lichess and allow the league to create your games (the challenge:write permission — nothing else).
  2. An admin reviews your application, usually within a few days.
  3. Once approved you're paired in the next round. You can pause from your account page at any time.

Fair play

  • Play your own moves. Opening books and databases are allowed, as on Lichess correspondence; engines and any other move assistance are not.
  • Play your games out. Two weeks in a row without starting a game pauses your quest until you resume it.
  • Be a decent opponent: the league runs on Lichess's terms of service and the Lichess4545 community norms.
